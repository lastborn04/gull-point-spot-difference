How to play

Compare the two pictures. Tap or click a difference in either view to mark it in both. With a keyboard, Tab to a picture, move with the arrow keys, and press Enter to inspect. Hold Shift for smaller steps.

In timed mode the clock starts on your first guess or hint. An incorrect guess costs four seconds. Switching modes starts the survey over.
